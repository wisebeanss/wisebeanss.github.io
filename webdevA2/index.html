What are the cognitive skills?

Cognitive skills are the brain's core mental abilities used to think, read, learn, remember. They are what your mind uses to process information, interact with the world, and solve problems.

Types of Cognitive Skills

  1. Memory
  2. Processing Speed
  3. Sensory Processing
  4. Attention

Habits that can improve cognitive functions

  • Physical Activity

    Regular physical activity can improve blood flow to the brain and support better memory, concentration, and mental alertness. Activities such as running, cycling, swimming, walking, or playing sports can be beneficial. Even short periods of exercise can help you feel more energetic and focused.

  • Get Enough Sleep

    Aim for around 7–9 hours of quality sleep each night. Sleep gives your brain time to rest, process information, and strengthen memories from the day. Getting enough sleep can also improve concentration, reaction time, problem-solving skills, and your ability to learn new information.

  • Eating a Balanced Diet

    Your brain needs a steady supply of energy and nutrients to function effectively. Eating a balanced diet containing fruits, vegetables, whole grains, nuts, protein, and healthy fats can support overall brain health. Drinking enough water is also important, as dehydration can make it harder to concentrate and think clearly.

Reaction Speed

What is Reaction Speed?

Reaction speed is how long it takes for a human to process and act upon receiving new information It involves your senses, nervous system, and your muscles working in unison, all happening in a fraction of a second

Types of Reaction Time

  1. Simple (reflex) Reaction Time: The time taken to respond to a single stimulus with a single response

  2. Choice Reaction Time: The time taken to consciously respond to one of several stimuli, each requiring a different response

Average Reaction Speed

The average reaction speed of a human is about 250 milliseconds while competitive e-sports professionals generally range around 150-170ms

Fun facts about the human reaction speed

  • Auditory signals reach the brain processing power faster than visual cues does, hence why sprinters react to the bang of the gun instead of its flash. However, you process touch the fastest, averaging around 0.15 seconds

  • Human reaction time generally peak around 24 years of age before declining by around 4 to 10 milliseconds after that

  • When attempting to anticipate and react to somemthing, your reaction time fluctuates because you occasionally lose focus before regaining it back after

Factors Influencing Reaction Time:

  1. Stimulus Type: Different types of stimuli (visual, auditory, tactile) can affect reaction time. Generally, people respond faster to auditory stimuli than to visual ones
  2. Stimulus Intensity: Stronger stimuli can lead to quicker reaction times.
  3. Complexity of Response: Simple tasks usually result in faster reaction times compared to complex tasks that require more processing.
  4. Individual Differences: Age, level of fatigue, and other individual factors can influence reaction time.
  5. State of Attention: Being more focused and attentive can reduce reaction time.
  6. Practice and Training: Repeated exposure and practice can improve reaction time.
  7. Environmental Factors: Conditions like lighting, noise, and distractions can affect reaction time.
